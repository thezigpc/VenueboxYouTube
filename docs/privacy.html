Privacy policy

Bar Games YouTube · last updated November 12, 2026

Bar Games YouTube ("the app") runs on a venue's own Bar Games box. Venue staff use it to send YouTube playlists to the venue's TVs. This policy explains what the app does with information, including information it receives from Google.

Information from your Google account

When venue staff choose "Sign in with Google", the app asks for read-only access to YouTube (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly). With it, the app reads:

  • the name of the YouTube channel signed in, to show which account is connected;
  • the account's playlists (titles, video counts, thumbnails), including private ones;
  • the videos in the playlists staff choose (video ids, titles, channel names, durations).

The app never uploads, edits or deletes anything on YouTube, and it does not read email, contacts or any other Google data.

How it is used and stored

  • The playlist information is used only to show staff their lists and to send the chosen videos to the venue's TVs.
  • It is stored only on the venue's own box, together with the Google sign-in token. Files holding tokens are readable by the app only.
  • It is not sold, shared with or sent to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising or to build profiles.
  • Guests see only the TV names and the titles of what is playing.

Bar Games YouTube's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and data

  • Staff can press "Sign out" on the app's Google page. This deletes the stored sign-in and asks Google to revoke it.
  • You can also remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Deleting a list in the app deletes its stored copy. Removing the app from the box deletes all of its data.
