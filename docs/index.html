Bar Games YouTube

Bar Games YouTube is an app on a venue's Bar Games box. Venue staff use it to pick YouTube playlists and play them on the venue's TVs. The videos play in each TV's own YouTube app, under the venue's YouTube account; this app works as the remote control and keeps the list of playlists.

Signing in with Google

Staff can sign in with the venue's Google account so the app can list that account's YouTube playlists. The app asks for read-only access (youtube.readonly). It never uploads, changes or deletes anything on YouTube.

It uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
